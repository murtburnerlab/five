1. Privacy Policy

FIVE is an independent artist-discovery project. This policy describes information processed through the FIVE website and its related features.

Who is responsible for the information?

The operator of FIVE is Artem Prokhorov, an individual based in Ukraine. Contact: hello@fourfive.art.

Information we process

  • Artist directory information: artist name, country, and a link to the artist’s public Instagram profile. The directory does not intentionally display artist photographs or artwork images.
  • Account information: account identifier and authentication information managed through our authentication provider; email address and any profile details you choose to provide.
  • Selections and activity: the FIVE round in which you participate, the artist you select, the time of the selection, and associated account identifiers. These records are used to operate the experience, maintain personal history where available, prevent abuse, and calculate displayed rankings and aggregate statistics.
  • Artist submissions: the artist name, country, Instagram link, optional website, contact email if supplied, submitter account identifier where available, submission status, and timestamps.
  • Basic analytics: a pseudonymous visitor identifier, event type (for example, page view, ranking view, round started, account login/signup, artist submission, or selection recorded), page path, associated account identifier where available, and timestamp.
  • Technical and security data: information that hosting, database, authentication, or security providers may process in their ordinary operation, such as request and error logs. The precise fields and retention periods depend on the relevant provider and configuration.

Why we use this information

  • To provide the FIVE artist-discovery experience, accounts, rounds, selections, rankings, and user history.
  • To review artist submissions, respond to requests, correct directory information, and prevent duplicate or abusive activity.
  • To understand aggregate site usage, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve the service.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and handle disputes.

Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide a feature you request, based on our legitimate interests in operating and securing an independent discovery service, based on consent where consent is required, or necessary to comply with a legal obligation. The appropriate basis depends on the specific processing and the jurisdiction. If you believe a particular use is inappropriate, contact us using the details below.

Public rankings and third-party links

FIVE may display aggregate counts and rankings derived from selections recorded on the platform. A public ranking does not reveal a visitor’s email address or account history. Artist Instagram links take you to a third-party service operated by Meta. Once you leave FIVE, that service’s privacy policy and practices apply.

Service providers and international processing

FIVE currently uses GitHub Pages for static website hosting and Supabase for database and authentication functionality. These providers may process information to provide their services. Depending on provider configuration and your location, information may be processed in other countries. Review the providers’ current terms and privacy documentation for details about their processing and safeguards.

Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including providing account features, maintaining reliable rankings, handling submissions and disputes, protecting the service, and meeting legal obligations. Retention can differ by data type. Deletion from the live database may not immediately remove copies held in backups or provider logs, which are handled under the relevant provider’s retention practices.

Operator action required: set and document concrete retention periods for analytics events, rejected submissions, and account-related records, and ensure the technical configuration follows those periods.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; or lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a request, contact hello@fourfive.art. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. If you have an account, use the account-deletion feature if available or contact us for assistance.

Children

FIVE is not designed specifically for children. Do not use the service or submit personal information if you are not legally permitted to do so under the laws applicable to you.

Security

We use the access controls and security features available in our service providers, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information to us by email.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

3. Artist Listing & Removal Policy

How listings work

FIVE may include an artist’s name, country of representation, and a link to a public Instagram profile for discovery purposes. We do not intentionally publish artist photographs or images of artworks as part of the directory. A listing is editorial and informational; it does not indicate endorsement, affiliation, or formal participation.

Corrections and removal requests

An artist, authorized representative, or other person with a legitimate concern may request a correction or removal by emailing hello@fourfive.art with the artist name, the relevant FIVE listing or Instagram URL, the requested change, and a brief explanation. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information unless reasonably necessary and requested through a safe process.

We will review requests in a reasonable time, may ask for clarification, and may temporarily hide a listing while reviewing a credible rights or safety concern. We may decline requests where we have a lawful basis to retain accurate public-interest information, subject to applicable law. We will explain the outcome where appropriate.

Report an issue

Use the same contact for broken links, incorrect country information, impersonation concerns, alleged unlawful content, privacy requests, or other listing issues. Include enough detail for us to identify the listing and understand the concern.

Submitting an artist

When submitting an artist, provide accurate information and only submit contact details or other information you are entitled to share. A submission does not guarantee inclusion. FIVE may review, reject, correct, or remove submissions according to its editorial criteria and applicable law.
