1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the FIVE website and its related features (the “Service”). FIVE is an independent artist-discovery project built around the concept “Five artists. One choice.” This Policy explains what information is processed, why it is processed, how it is shared and retained, and how to make a privacy request.

1.1 Who is responsible?

The data controller and operator of FIVE is Artem Prokhorov, an individual based in Ukraine (“FIVE”, “we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy matters, contact hello@fourfive.art. Please include “Privacy request” in the subject line. This contact information does not imply that FIVE is a separate company or legal entity.

1.2 Information we process

  • Account and authentication data: your account identifier, email address, authentication/session information and account status, handled through our authentication/database provider.
  • Choices and account history: the artist selected, the round or mode in which a selection is made, timestamps, and the account identifier associated with the selection. These records operate the core Service, support personal history where available, and calculate rankings and aggregate counts.
  • Artist directory information: artist name, country, and public profile links, such as Instagram and, where provided, a website link. The directory is intended to provide links and identifying information; it does not intentionally host artists’ photographs or artwork images.
  • Artist submissions and requests: artist name, country, Instagram URL, optional website, contact email if supplied, submitter/account identifier where available, review status, timestamps, and information included in correspondence or a correction/removal request.
  • Optional analytics: if you accept analytics, FIVE may process a pseudonymous visitor identifier, event type, page path, timestamp and, where available, an account identifier. Events may include page views and interactions with Service features. Analytics are intended to help understand aggregate use, troubleshoot and improve the Service.
  • Operational and security data: hosting, database, authentication and content-delivery providers may generate or process technical information such as request metadata, diagnostics, error logs and security records as part of providing their services. The precise data and retention periods are controlled in part by those providers.
  • Messages to us: the sender address and the contents of any email or other communication you send to the operator.

Please do not send passwords, identity documents or sensitive personal information unless it is strictly necessary and we have agreed on a safe way to provide it.

1.3 Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information on the following bases, as relevant to the particular activity:

  • Providing the Service: to create and authenticate accounts, record requested selections, display personal history, calculate rankings and show aggregate statistics. Where applicable, this is necessary to provide the feature you request or perform the user-facing arrangement for that feature.
  • Operating and protecting the Service: to review submissions, prevent abuse, maintain integrity of rankings, troubleshoot errors, secure systems and respond to claims. Where applicable, this is based on our legitimate interests in operating a reliable artist-discovery service, balanced against the rights and interests of affected individuals.
  • Optional analytics: based on your choice/consent where required by applicable law. If you reject analytics, the optional analytics events described in Section 2 should not be sent by the Service. Your choice of an artist remains a core feature and may still be recorded to operate the ranking and your account history.
  • Legal compliance and claims: where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Public artist information: to provide an editorial directory using limited identifying information and public profile links. We consider the context, necessity and potential impact of such inclusion and provide a process to request correction or removal.

The exact legal basis depends on the processing activity and the law that applies to you. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time using the available privacy settings or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not make prior lawful processing unlawful and does not affect processing that has a separate lawful basis.

1.4 Core choices are different from optional analytics

A selection made in FIVE is part of the Service itself. If you use an account to choose an artist, the selection may be stored and counted to operate the feature, maintain your history, prevent abuse and calculate rankings. Rejecting optional analytics does not mean that the selection itself is anonymous or that the core selection record is not processed. It means that optional analytics tracking should be disabled as described in Section 2.

1.5 Public rankings and visibility

FIVE may publish aggregate rankings and counts derived from recorded selections. Public ranking information is intended to describe activity within FIVE, not to publish your email address or personal account history. We do not intentionally publish a visitor’s individual selection history or account email as part of the public ranking. Information you independently publish on a third-party profile remains subject to that third party’s settings and policies.

1.7 International processing

Our operator is based in Ukraine, and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located, depending on their infrastructure and configuration. Data-protection rules differ between countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the transfer must rely on an appropriate mechanism under that law. 1.8 Retention

  • Optional analytics events: the FIVE database cleanup configuration is intended to delete analytics event records older than 200 days. Deletion depends on the scheduled cleanup operating successfully.
  • Rejected artist submissions: the cleanup configuration is intended to delete rejected submissions only when both the submission date and the review date (or submission date if no review date exists) are more than 200 days old. This supports the existing resubmission/cooldown process. Deletion depends on the scheduled cleanup operating successfully.
  • Accounts, selections and history: these records may be retained while needed to provide account features, maintain ranking integrity, prevent abuse, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. We do not state a fixed deletion period for these records in this Policy because a complete, verified lifecycle for every related record has not been established here. You may request account/data deletion using the contact details in Section 1.10; some records may need to be retained where a lawful basis or technical requirement applies.
  • Provider logs, backups and email: copies may remain for periods set by the relevant provider, backup cycle or email system, subject to applicable law and provider settings.

We do not promise that deletion from the live database immediately removes every backup, log or copy held by a service provider. We will take reasonable steps within our control to apply an appropriate retention and deletion process.

We use available access controls and security features provided by the Service’s infrastructure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for keeping access to your email account and devices secure. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly.

2. Analytics & Consent

2.1 Your choice

When the consent prompt is shown, you can accept or reject optional analytics. The Service also provides a Privacy Settings control so you can revisit your choice, where that control is available. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under the key five_analytics_consent. This is browser storage, not a traditional tracking cookie. Clearing browser data or using another browser/device may reset the choice.

2.2 If you reject analytics

Optional analytics events should not be sent to the FIVE analytics function when your stored choice is not “accepted”. This includes the additional call_cast event associated with a successful artist selection, provided the current deployed code includes the consent check. The selection itself may still be stored as a core Service record to calculate rankings and support account history. Infrastructure providers may still process necessary technical/security logs as part of hosting and providing the Service.

2.3 If you accept analytics

FIVE may send the optional analytics events described in Section 1.2 to help measure aggregate use and improve the Service. Acceptance of optional analytics is not a condition for making a choice or using core discovery functionality.

2.4 Changing or withdrawing your choice

You can change your choice through the Privacy Settings control where available. If you withdraw consent, future optional analytics events should stop. Withdrawal does not automatically erase event records collected before withdrawal; those records are subject to the retention schedule and applicable law. You may separately request deletion where you have a right to do so.

Browser storage controls the Service’s optional analytics setting. This statement does not guarantee that every third-party infrastructure log is disabled when analytics are rejected.

4. Artist Listing & Removal Policy

4.1 How listings work

FIVE may include an artist’s name, country and a link to a public Instagram profile and, where available, a website. Listings are editorial and informational and do not imply endorsement, affiliation, approval or formal participation. FIVE does not intentionally publish artist photographs or artwork images as part of the directory.

4.2 Corrections and removal requests

An artist, authorized representative or person with a legitimate concern may request a correction or removal by emailing hello@fourfive.art. Include the artist’s name, the relevant FIVE listing or profile URL, the requested change, and a brief explanation. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive information unless reasonably necessary and requested through a safe process.

We will review requests within a reasonable period and may ask for clarification or evidence of authority where appropriate. We may temporarily hide a listing while reviewing a credible privacy, impersonation, safety or rights concern. We will consider accuracy, privacy, public availability, applicable law and the information provided. Where lawful and appropriate, we will communicate the outcome. A request does not guarantee removal in every case, but we will assess it in good faith and comply with applicable law.

4.3 Report an issue

Use the same contact for broken links, incorrect country information, impersonation, alleged unlawful content, privacy concerns or other listing issues. Include enough detail to identify the relevant listing and understand the concern. We may preserve relevant records where necessary to investigate or meet legal obligations.

4.4 Submitting an artist

When submitting an artist, provide accurate information and only submit contact details or other information you are entitled to share. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information. A submission does not guarantee inclusion. FIVE may review, reject, correct, defer or remove submissions under its editorial criteria, operational requirements and applicable law. Information about a submission is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
