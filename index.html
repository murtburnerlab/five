ART DESERVES
TO BE DISCOVERED.

A new way to discover contemporary artists — and make your own voice part of the picture.

WHY FIVE EXISTS

Great artists are everywhere. Finding them shouldn't depend on knowing the right people. FIVE gives people a simple way to discover artists, look at their work, and express what resonates with them.

02 / MORE THAN A RANKING

DISCOVERY IS
THE POINT.

A ranking is one part of FIVE. The bigger idea is to make it easier to encounter artists you might never have found otherwise, follow your own taste, and take part in a growing public record of people's choices.

THE PRINCIPLE

PEOPLE
DECIDE.
NOT CONNECTIONS.

Everyone brings a different eye, background, and point of view. FIVE records real choices and makes those choices visible. It does not claim there is one objectively best artist — it shows what people choose, and how those choices change.

02 / HOW DISCOVERY WORKS

VISIBILITY
IS NOT THE SAME
AS POPULARITY.

FIVE is designed to keep discovery moving. An artist who has just been approved is new to the system, so the platform gives that artist more opportunities to be encountered than artists who have already been shown repeatedly. As artists enter the system and people make choices, the discovery mix evolves.

THE PRINCIPLE

NEW NAMES
DESERVE A
REAL CHANCE.

The public ranking records people's choices; it does not decide what anyone should like. Exposure creates an opportunity to be considered, not a guaranteed vote. Every person remains free to choose the artist they genuinely prefer.

03 / WHY IT MATTERS

YOUR TASTE.
YOUR DECISION.

“People decide. Not connections.”

FIVE is not here to tell people which artist is objectively the best. It is here to help people find artists they might otherwise miss, trust their own response to the work, and make those choices visible. For artists, that means a chance to be discovered outside their existing audience. For the public, it means more possibilities and a more personal way into contemporary art.
